About the firm

A focused legal practice for commercial and private client work.

Sterling & Vale combines partner-led strategy with practical matter handling, giving clients direct access to legal advice that is structured, clear, and prepared for pressure.

Our story

Founded to bring senior legal attention closer to each matter.

The firm began in 2011 after its founding partners saw too many clients passed between departments without a clear plan. Sterling & Vale was built around direct partner accountability, concise matter strategy, and legal documents that are written for real business and family decisions.

Today the firm advises companies, property investors, trustees, families, and individuals across commercial law, litigation, estates, trusts, and conveyancing.
