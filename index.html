Brief history

Built for legal work where timing, evidence, and judgment matter.

Sterling & Vale was established in 2011 by commercial litigators who wanted a tighter, more responsive legal practice for owner-managed companies, executives, families, and property clients.

15+ years senior partner experience
24h urgent enquiry response target
1 direct matter team per client
2011

Commercial dispute practice founded

The firm opened with a focus on contract disputes, shareholder conflict, and urgent business matters.

2016

Private client and estates team added

Sterling & Vale expanded to support families, trustees, executors, and succession planning clients.

2022

Property and conveyancing desk launched

The firm added transfer, lease, and development support for property investors and business owners.
