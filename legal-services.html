What to send

Useful intake prompts help the firm respond faster.

A strong contact page can collect the context the attorney needs before the first call, without overwhelming the client.

A

Parties involved

Names of companies, individuals, trustees, directors, sellers, buyers, or opposing parties.

B

Documents available

Contracts, emails, invoices, title deeds, wills, trust documents, pleadings, or correspondence.

C

Urgency and outcome

Deadlines, court dates, threatened action, signing dates, or the result the client wants.

Not sure which service fits?

Send a short summary and the firm can route the enquiry to the correct legal department.
